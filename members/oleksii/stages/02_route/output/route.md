### 1.2 The transition channel from TP to ordinary residence
- Disposición adicional 19ª of **RD 1155/2024** established the basis for a TP→ordinary-authorization channel. As of Feb 2026 the BOE itself noted the pasarela was "not yet regulated" (see the Preamble of INT/96/2026 text). **RD 316/2026 (14/04/2026)** modified DA 19ª (and other provisions) to make it operative, and **Instrucciones SEM 2/2026** (Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones, signed **22/06/2026**) laid down the procedure. Key content relevant to Oleks:

  - **Instrucción 6ª — larga duración nacional**: the time in TP **counts in full** toward the 5-year legal stay:

    > «…el tiempo durante el que una persona haya sido beneficiaria de protección temporal en España computa íntegramente a efectos del cumplimiento del plazo de cinco años de residencia legal y continuada exigido» (larga duración nacional).
    > — Instrucciones SEM 2/2026, Instrucción Sexta (full text PDF, Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones).

  - **Instrucción 1ª / 2ª — ordinary residence-y-trabajo via art. 191**: TP holders may apply for modification to residence & work (art. 191 Reglamento) **without** the limitation of art. 191.7; and arraigo (art. 125) is available even while holding TP (art. 126.h doesn't exclude; the 2-year stay counts via art. 126.b).
    - **Duration of the art. 191 permit — 4 años**: when the modified authorization already habilitaba a trabajar (Oleksii's TP card shows AUTORIZA A TRABAJAR) and the applicant has ≥1 year en situación de residencia, the new residencia y trabajo authorization has a **4-year validity** (art. 191.3 RD 1155/2024, second paragraph — «la vigencia de la nueva autorización tendrá una duración de cuatro años»). SEM 1/2026 applies the same rule to the humanitarian→art.191 route. Verified live 2026-09-10 against the reglamento text + intergroupabogados + infomigrante analysis (Kagi search).
  - **Renuncia (waiver)**: the transition is **not automatic** — you apply, and **after** the new authorization is granted you must expressly waive TP (**art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003**), which you prove when you request the new TIE. Waiving TP before grant would create a gap.
  - Minors (arts. 159–160) are exempt from the 2-year stay and (art. 160) from economic means and housing — relevant for Artem, not for Oleks.

### 1.3 Residencia de larga duración — the base rule
- Article **32 LO 4/2000**; procedure in **Título X, artículos 175 a 189, RD 1155/2024** (Vademecum Legal, rev. 23/02/2026).
- Requirements: **5 years of legal and continuous residence** (TP time counts per SEM 2/2026), no criminal record in Spain + countries of last 5 years, sustainable economic means, not returning. No continuity breaks >6 months continuous / >10 total in 5 years (labor reasons: up to 18 months).
- Form **EX-11**, tasa **790-052 ep. 2.6** (≈21.80 €). Decision in 3 months, **positive silence**. After grant: apply for TIE within 1 month (EX-17 + tasa 790-012 ≈ 16.08 €).

2. Select "Modificación de autorización de residencia temporal" (art. 191).
3. Upload scanned documents to each field.
4. Complete the EX-25 modification form.
5. Generate the tasación (790-052), pay online via bank, upload receipt.
6. Submit the application — obtain a resguardo (receipt number).

**Phase 3: Processing & resolution**
1. Wait for resolution (typically 3 months; positive silence after 3 months).
2. If art. 191 approved, receive "autorización de residencia y trabajo" (4-year permit).
3. Waive TP formally at the next opportunity (when applying for the new TIE).
4. Apply for new TIE card at your local extranjería office (bring all docs + resguardo).
5. Collect TIE at the specified date.

**Timeline estimate:** 4–6 months total (filing → resolution → TIE).
