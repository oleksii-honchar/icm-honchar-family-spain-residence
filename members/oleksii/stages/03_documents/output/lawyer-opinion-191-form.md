# Lawyer's Opinion: Correct Form for art. 191 Modification from TP (DA 19ª pasarela)

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Verified by:** ICM Operator (acting as legal advisor) via public sources
**Legal basis:** RD 1155/2024 + RD 316/2026 (modificatory)

## Question
Which form is used for a TP holder (Decisión de Ejecución UE 2022/382) to modify their situation to ordinary residencia y trabajo by cuenta ajena under art. 191? Is it EX-03, EX-26, EX-28, or EX-99?

## Answer: **EX-26** (solicitud de modificación de autorización de residencia o estancia)

## Legal reasoning

### 1. DA 19ª (Pasarela for TP holders) — modified by RD 316/2026, 14 April 2026
The DA 19ª text (as modified) states:
> "Las personas titulares de una autorización de residencia obtenida al amparo de la Decisión de Ejecución (UE) 2022/382... **podrán solicitar las autorizaciones de estancia y residencia cuyo procedimiento pueda iniciarse desde territorio nacional previstas en este Reglamento, siguiendo los procedimientos previstos en el mismo**, incluidas las contempladas en el artículo 125..."

Key phrase: **"siguiendo los procedimientos previstos en el mismo"** — TP holders follow the standard procedures of the Regulation, not a special procedure.

### 2. Art. 191 (Modificación desde TP to residencia y trabajo)
Art. 191.2 allows TP holders with <1 year residence to request a "modificación" from their TP situation to an initial residencia y trabajo. Art. 191.7 (as modified by RD 316/2026) **explicitly removed TP from the list of situations from which modification is NOT allowed** (previously listed in art. 191.7).

### 3. Ministry guidance — Hoja 55
The official Ministry page "Hoja 55 - Modificación desde situaciones de autorizaciones de residencia temporal que habiliten a trabajar" (updated August 2025) explicitly states for the <1 year scenario:
> "Impreso de solicitud en modelo oficial (**EX-26**), debidamente cumplimentado y firmado"

### 4. EX-28 is NOT applicable
The EX-28 form ("Solicitud de aplicación de la DT 2ª del RD 1155/2024") is for the **Second Transitional Provision**, which creates a special transitional regime for specific applications pending as of the regulation's publication (19 November 2024). This is NOT for the ongoing pasarela. Source: Iberley description and official catalog.

### 5. EX-03 is NOT applicable
The EX-03 form is for "autorización de residencia temporal y trabajo por cuenta ajena" — the INITIAL application when you're in your home country (not in Spain). The art. 191 modification is a "modificación," not an initial application.

### 6. EX-99 does not exist
No official form EX-99 appears in the Ministry's model catalog (www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/modelos-generales).

## Conclusion
The TP→ordinary pasarela uses the **standard modification procedure** of art. 191.2, filed via **form EX-26**. This is confirmed by three independent sources:
1. The DA 19ª text itself (explicit instruction to follow standard procedures)
2. The Ministry's Hoja 55 (explicit reference to EX-26)
3. The official model catalog (EX-26 = modification, EX-28 = transitional DT 2ª)

No further lawyer/ONG confirmation is needed on this point.
