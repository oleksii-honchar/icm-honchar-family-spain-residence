# 02_route — choose authorization route

**Inputs**
- `01_profile/output/profile.md` (this member)
- `_shared/rules.md` (legal framework, as of Sep 2026)

**Process**
1. Classify the member (minor / adult-with-work / adult-spouse) per the roster.
2. Map candidate routes from `_shared/rules.md` (minors: art. 160 EX-25; adults: work-route
   ordinary residence vs EX-11 larga duración; non-working: arraigo familiar).
3. For each candidate: form, tasa, timing window, ties to other members, risks.
4. Pick one route with rationale; list open legal questions needing ONG/lawyer confirmation.

**Outputs** → `output/route.md` (chosen route + rationale + open questions)

**Human check** — decide the route here (confirm with ONG/lawyer before 03_documents).
