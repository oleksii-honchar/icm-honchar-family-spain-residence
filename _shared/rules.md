## Transition mechanisms

- **DA 19ª RD 1155/2024**: TP holders may apply for ordinary residence authorizations **from within Spain** without renouncing TP first.
- **Instrucción SEM 2/2026** (signed 22/06/2026, Secretaría de Estado de Migraciones): TP holders may transition to ordinary authorizations *without renouncing TP first*; TP time **counts in full** toward the 5 years of legal+continuous residence for **larga duración NACIONAL** (Instrucción Sexta), toward arraigo (2y, art. 125 + 126.b), and minors (arts. 159–160) get exemptions (2y permanence + medios/housing for art. 160). Supported on DA 19ª as amended by **RD 316/2026** (14/04/2026). Transition is NOT automatic — you must apply, and upon grant you must expressly waive TP (art. 24.1.d RD 1325/2003), proved when applying for the new TIE. Larga duración: **art. 32 LO 4/2000 + Título X, arts. 175–189 RD 1155/2024**. Does NOT count for larga duración-UE (Directive 2003/109). Administrative instruction, not judicially settled — confirm with ONG/lawyer.
- **Card validity**: TIE auto-extended to **04/03/2027** by **Orden INT/96/2026** (BOE 17/02/2026); no new card needed for art. 209 obligations. TP regime to **04/03/2028** (Decisión (UE) 2026/1912, adopted 30/07/2026). Also note: the BOE text of INT/96/2026 (Feb 2026) recorded that the DA 19ª "pasarela" was *not yet regulated* — that gap was later filled by RD 316/2026 (14/04/2026) + SEM 2/2026 (22/06/2026).
- **Duration of the art. 191 residencia y trabajo authorization = 4 años**: when the modified authorization already habilitaba trabajar and the applicant has ≥1 year in a residence situation, the new authorization runs **four years** (art. 191.3 RD 1155/2024, second para). Applies to the protection-temporal transition (SEM 2/2026) and the humanitarian-route transition (SEM 1/2026). Verified 2026-09-10 (reglamento text + lawyer analysis). This corrects the earlier "1–2y, renewable" assumption in `oleksii/02_route/output/route.md`.

## Larga duración nacional — requirements (Hoja 49 / arts 182–185)

- 5 years legal + **continuous** residence (TP time counts per SEM 2/2026).
- No criminal record in Spain + countries of last 5 years (certificado de antecedentes penales). **NOTE**: for art. 191 modifications, art. 74.h) only requires Spanish criminal record ("inexistencia de antecedentes penales en España") — foreign certificates are NOT required. Foreign certs only needed for EX-11 larga duración (art. 177.3.f).
- Not EU/EEA/CH citizen; not rejected; no return commitment.
- **Form EX-11**, tasa 790-052 ep. 2.6 (≈€21.80). Resolution 3 months, positive silence.
- After grant: within 1 month apply in person for **TIE** at police station (EX-17 + tasa 790-012 ≈ €16.08 first card; larga duración TIE ≈ €21.87).
- Absences: max 6 months continuous / 10 months total in 5 years (labor reasons: up to 18 months).
- **Do NOT wait until TP ends (Mar 2028)** — transition takes months; start early.
